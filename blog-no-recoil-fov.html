No Recoil Control and Aim FOV Customization

No recoil control is how you keep sprays on target. Aim FOV customization is how you set how wide the aimbot looks. Together they shape how Advanced Aimbot feels in real Delta Force fights.

One stabilizes the gun. The other sets the pickup radius. Tune both and prediction stops feeling sticky or weak.

Achieve Perfect Accuracy with No Recoil Control

Every weapon in Delta Force has vertical and horizontal kick. No recoil control reduces or removes that kick so your crosshair stays on the bone you picked while you fire.

That matters on full-auto rifles, SMGs, and long sprays. With no recoil on, more of the magazine lands where aimbot prediction put you.

Optimize Your Gameplay with Aim FOV Customization

Aim FOV is the radius around your crosshair where the aimbot can pick up a target. Small FOV only helps when you are already close. Large FOV grabs farther off-center.

Too wide feels sticky. Too narrow misses easy tracks. Dial it to how you already aim — not to your camera FOV. Camera FOV is the game view. Aim FOV is only the lock radius.

Using No Recoil and FOV Together

  • Start with a medium FOV and test in real matches
  • Turn no recoil on for your main weapons
  • Lower FOV if you want more manual control
  • Raise FOV slightly for smoke and CQC

Change one setting at a time. Full presets are in the Aimbot Setup Guide. Pair with Player ESP so you see the peek before the lock grabs.

What does no recoil control do in Delta Force?

Aim FOV is the radius around your crosshair where the aimbot can grab a target. Small FOV is tighter. Large FOV grabs farther off-center. Tune it to your playstyle.

Is aim FOV the same as camera FOV?
No. Camera FOV is how wide the game view is. Aim FOV is only the aimbot pickup radius. Tune them separately.
