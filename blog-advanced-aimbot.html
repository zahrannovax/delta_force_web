What Advanced Aimbot Does

Advanced Aimbot helps your crosshair track and land shots on enemy players with far more consistency than manual aim alone. In Delta Force, that matters in close rooms, long lanes, and fights where targets slide behind cover or move through smoke.

Bone Targeting and Prediction

Bone targeting lets the aimbot lock to a chosen body point — head, chest, or other bones — based on your settings. That gives you control over how aggressive or natural the shots feel.

Trajectory prediction is what makes the difference on moving targets. The aimbot reads target movement and leads the shot so bullets meet the player where they will be, not where they were a moment ago. That is especially useful in Operations when enemies sprint between cover or ride vehicles.
